# Privacy policy

The privacy policy of the Randnotiz app is published on
**[randnotiz.info](https://randnotiz.info)**. The German version is the original;
the translations are provided for convenience.

| Language | Privacy policy |
|---|---|
| Deutsch (original) | <https://randnotiz.info/de/datenschutz/> |
| English | <https://randnotiz.info/en/privacy/> |
| Français | <https://randnotiz.info/fr/confidentialite/> |
| Español | <https://randnotiz.info/es/privacidad/> |

The app links to the version in its own language: Settings → "Privacy policy".

## In short

- Randnotiz has **no server of its own, no account, no ads and no tracking**.
- Notes, moods, places and songs are stored only on your device, in an **encrypted
  database** whose key never leaves the device. Android's cloud backup is disabled.
- Randnotiz only sees the photos you pick in Android's photo picker – it has no
  access to your gallery.
- Only what a feature needs goes online: weather and place search (Open-Meteo, with a
  position rounded to about 1 km or your search term), possibly Google's speech
  recognition when dictating, the song search at Apple (only when you add a song) and
  – only if you turn it on – the **end-to-end encrypted** backup in your own Google
  Drive.

This summary is not the policy itself; if in doubt, the policy on randnotiz.info
applies.
